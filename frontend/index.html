What the AI does

It plans. You decide.

Ollama turns your origin, destination, dates and travel style into a real itinerary with timings, providers and costs. The progress you see while it works is tied to the actual request — we never tick off a step that has not happened.

  • Distances and cab fares come from live map data.
  • If a fare cannot be fetched, TripMind says so instead of guessing.
  • If the model’s reply is cut short, complete parts are recovered instead of failing the trip.
